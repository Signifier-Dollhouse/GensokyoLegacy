# Party — known limits and resolved questions

## 1. Known limits

- **`tea_table` and `magic_table` cannot host parties.** Deliberate (food_mods.md §6). A small indoor party needs a second pass.
- **Farmer's Delight and Youkai's Feasts are `compileOnly` in the dev build.** Their jars sit in the `libs/` flatDir and resolve for compilation, but neither is loaded at runtime. Adding either as an `implementation` dependency puts a second copy of the L2 libraries on the module path and the dev launcher dies during class-layer construction with a `java.lang.module.ResolutionException` about split packages — before any mod code runs, so no amount of code changes avoids it. Consequence for testing: a dev run has **no modded party food**, only `minecraft:cake`, and the `required: false` tag entries resolve to nothing. The party is fully playable and testable that way; testing the FD/YSF foods needs a real instance with the mods installed.
- **A malformed id in a tag provider hangs `runData`, silently** (area.md §1). `GensokyoLegacy.loc()` namespaces a bare path with our modid, so passing it a foreign id produces a path containing a colon; the provider then never returns, with no error and no stack trace, and it looks exactly like a deadlock in Registrate. Foreign ids must go through `ResourceLocation.parse`.
- **`YSF_WOODS` is a hand-kept list in `GLTagGen`.** It exists because a tag provider must not touch `GLDecoBlocks`, and it must be kept in step with `WoodType` by hand.
- **A party can only be sent on a `party_table`,** so the letter is aimed at the table rather than at the food. Clicking a food block directly does nothing.
- **Reputation is sampled once, at send.** Reputation earned during the party does not upgrade the roster and reputation lost during it evicts nobody. A guest who turns hostile mid-party keeps eating. A consequence of freezing the roster, and the reason `/gensokyo party preview` can be exact.
- **Guest eating is not interruptible.** If the player breaks the food out from under a guest she re-validates and picks again, but she cannot be redirected to a different dish.
- **Chunk unload freezes the party mid-feast.** Guests stop ticking with the chunk and resume on reload. Acceptable — the same behaviour as every other entity in the chunk — but a party in a chunk the player walks away from simply pauses.
- **A guest belongs to the chunk that owns the party, not the chunk she stands in.** Both the anchor table and the food centre lie inside the walk's reach, so a party routinely straddles a chunk border. Guests therefore carry the owning `ChunkPos` explicitly and look the party up by that — never by `new ChunkPos(table)`, which silently misses and makes her dismiss herself on her first tick. Same reasoning behind `/gensokyo party info|end` searching the neighbourhood through `PartyAttachment.near` instead of one chunk.
- **A guest keeps her chair for the visit, and loses it only to a broken chair.** guest.md §2.1: the party's seating plan is first-write-wins, so she dismounts to walk to a dish beyond her reach and comes back to the same one, and a rearranged table cannot walk her off it. `HomeSearchUtil.setSitting` is a one-shot call with no un-sit counterpart, so the dismount is an explicit `stopRiding` and the claim is the party's own record — which also means a guest whose chair is destroyed while she is away is re-seated by the normal rule rather than by anything special.
- **The host's reputation payout needs the host online.** send.md §4: `GLMeta.CHAR` is a player attachment, so a host who logs off or changes dimension before her guests leave is paid nothing for that party. This is the same rule as the party's messages and for the same reason, but it is a loss of a reward rather than of a notification, which is the part worth knowing.
- **No cross-dimension concern.** A guest clone spawns in the host's level and `party_taste` is dimension-independent, so any character may be invited anywhere. Noted only so it is not rediscovered as a bug later.
- **A guest's seat and dish search is a fixed 6-block box, not `PartyArea.scan`.** `PartyArea.scan` walks a whole room; this is a triple loop over `GUEST_RADIUS`, swept by the **party** on a timer (guest.md §1) rather than by each guest for herself, because a guest asks for the list every time she draws a dish and that is every few seconds for the length of her visit. A banquet laid out over tens of blocks is not something a guest walks to and back anyway. This is the one place the scan distance deliberately does not apply, and the reason the scan radius and the guest radius are two different numbers.
- **A guest leaves when her `leaveChance` comes up, so a big table empties on its own.** A satisfied guest rolls 5% after each 5-second sit, which in simulation means a guest facing a 200-dish spread leaves after about eleven dishes — always by the roll, never by running the spread out. That is the intended shape and it is also a real tuning decision: 5% per sit is tuned so a guest lingers about a hundred seconds of sitting before wandering off, and a datapack author who wants a longer party raises `fullSeat` *or* lowers `leaveChance`, which are two different flavours of the same dial. `partyGuestRadius` keeps everyone inside the room but does not affect this. guest.md §2.3 makes the second half explicit: the last guest out ends the **party**, with the spread untouched.
- **Only one rig has a chew, and only two renderers draw a held item.** guest.md §1: `吃` exists for `PlainFairyEntity` (event 80), and `YoukaiHeldItemLayer` is registered by `CirnoRenderer` and `PlainFairyRenderer` only — so of the six characters in `party_taste`, one would visibly carry a slice and none would chew it. Both are one-line-per-renderer fixes, and both are art-side.
- **A reused character restores her wander area from her home, not from a saved value.** `restrictTo` has no getter, so `PartyModule.restoreWander` re-applies it from the `IHomeHolder` on the way out (guest.md §2.3). If a datapack or a structure change makes her home unreadable while she is away, the restore silently does nothing and she keeps the party's guest radius until her next chunk unload; a resident who cannot path home is the visible symptom.
- **A visitor whose window closes at a party never gets her walk back.** guest.md §2.3: she finishes leaving at the table and is given `hold(200)`. That is the intended ending and it means a visitor cannot be seen returning to a house that is no longer having her — but it also means nothing watches her walk, so if the hold expires while a player is watching she is removed by the visit module's own "nobody can see me" rule rather than by anything party-side.
- **The area draw is wand-only, and scan-only.** It never renders for a player not holding a party wand, so it is debug output with no gameplay footprint. It is also not refreshed by a real send (debug.md §3) — only by a wand click and by `preview`.
- **`en_ud` lang variants** regenerate with the rest; zh_cn entries go under `src/test/resources/gensokyolegacy/lang/zh_cn/` and the `ResourceOrganizer` main is rerun afterwards.

## 2. Resolved questions

Owner answers, 2026-10-06.

1. **`tea_table` / `magic_table`** — excluded from the feature. `party_table` is built from `LARGE_TABLE` only, and neither block is modified. Note that they needed no support workaround regardless: `large_table` already passes both survival checks unmodified (food_mods.md §6).
2. **Overlapping parties** — rejected by chunk scan. The party's owning chunk is the one holding the centre of the detected food; every chunk the food touches is scanned for an existing live party before a send is allowed.
3. **Chairs are a hard requirement** — zero seats rejects the send outright. A new `party_seat` tag carries our chairs and stools plus optional third-party seats; seating itself reuses `HomeSearchUtil.setSitting`, which no-ops on blocks that are not `ISeatableBlock`.
4. **Cost** — food only. No cooldown, no reputation gain or loss. Gifts are the whole return, sized on how much appeal that guest personally consumed (send.md §4).
5. **No letter cooldown** — the letter names no character, so there is nothing to rate-limit; a repeated send simply re-runs the same seeded draw.
6. **Cross-dimension guests** — no issue.
7. **Eligibility lives in `party_taste`** (later decision, reversing the earlier fallback). A character with no entry does not come to parties at all; `PartyTaste.of` returns null and `PartySend.plan` skips her. There is no separate whitelist, so the data map is the single place that says who is invitable, and an unwritten character is absent by decision rather than accidentally. `PartyTaste.DEFAULT` remains only as a template to copy — nothing reads it at runtime. See select.md §2.
